What LingapPH does

LingapPH is an information portal. It does not process applications, determine eligibility, approve benefits, or distribute government assistance.

Visitors can use LingapPH to:

  • Explore assistance programs by category
  • Review common eligibility requirements and documents
  • Find the official agency or office to contact
