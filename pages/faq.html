Frequently asked questions

Find quick answers about using LingapPH and verifying public assistance information.

What is LingapPH?

LingapPH is an information portal that helps people find and understand public assistance programs.

View answer

Does LingapPH process applications?

No. Applications, eligibility decisions, approvals, and benefit distribution are handled by the responsible agency.

View answer

How do I know if I qualify for assistance?

Review the official program requirements and contact the responsible agency for an eligibility decision.

View answer

What documents are usually required?

Requirements vary, but agencies may request valid identification, proof of need, and supporting records.

View answer

Where should I submit my application?

Submit applications through the official channel of the agency or local office responsible for the program.

View answer

How can I report incorrect information?

Contact the responsible agency directly and include the program name, incorrect detail, and an official source if available.
